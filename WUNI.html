I am a dedicated nursing researcher and educator currently pursuing a PhD in Nursing at the University of Kentucky. With extensive clinical experience across Ghana, the UK, and the US, my research focuses on patient safety, healthcare disparities, nursing education, and improving healthcare outcomes in underserved populations.

My work bridges clinical practice with rigorous academic research, with publications in reputable journals including BMC Nursing, Nursing Open, PLOS ONE, and Healthcare. I am passionate about mentoring the next generation of nurses and advancing evidence-based nursing practice.
